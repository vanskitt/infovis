How I cleaned the dataset

The raw data was exported directly from Spotify and covers the last 7 months of listening history. Before building the visualizations, two filters were applied to reduce noise:

01
Remove short streams

Entries with less than 0.5 minutes played were removed — these are skipped tracks that don't represent real listening.

−293 records removed 1,879 → 1,586
02
Keep only recurring artists

Artists streamed 5 times or fewer were excluded, keeping only those with a meaningful presence in my listening history.

−48 artists removed 1,586 → 1,495 records
After both filters: 1,495 records across 16 artists.
